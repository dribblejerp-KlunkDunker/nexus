# Deprecated synthetic captures — do not move these back

These PCAPs were generated by `feature_extractor.generate_synthetic_datasets()`
and `train_for_real.build_comprehensive_corpus()`. They are kept only so past
results can be reproduced.

**Why they are out of the globbed directories.**
Their benign class uses `ttl ∈ {64, 128}`. Real inbound packets arrive
decremented — 46–54 after a dozen hops — which is the same band these files
label as *attack*. Any model trained on them learns `|ttl − 64| large → attack`
and scores roughly half of real home traffic as hostile. The measured figure on
`home_live_baseline.pcap` was 55.29% of packets flagged.

`scripts/evolve.py` globs `data/normal_traffic/*.pcap` and
`data/attack_samples/*.pcap`, and that loader is shared by `continuous_loop.py`,
`dashboard.py` and `distributed_ray_evolve.py`. Leaving these files in place
meant any background evolution cycle could quietly re-learn the fault and
promote it over a good champion.

Training data now comes from `data/corpus_v2.npz`, built by
`scripts/build_corpus_v2.py` from real captures.

Attack samples may be re-introduced to a corpus build deliberately — the
problem is the synthetic *benign* class, not the attack side. Do that through
`build_corpus_v2.py`, which injects attacks into a real timeline and audits for
single-feature shortcuts, not by copying files back here.
